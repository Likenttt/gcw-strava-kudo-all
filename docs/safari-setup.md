# Kudo All in three steps

[简体中文](https://kudoall.li2niu.com/help/safari/zh/) · [Features](https://kudoall.li2niu.com/safari/)

**Mac Safari · macOS 12+ · One-time purchase, no subscription**. Local store price applies.

## 1. Download and open

[Download from the Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6458730808?pt=125713495&ct=kudo_safari_readme&mt=8). Then open **Garmin Connect Web Kudo All** once from Applications.

## 2. Enable and allow

In **Safari → Settings → Extensions** (older versions: **Preferences**), enable **Kudo All: Strava & Garmin**. Open your site, click the extension icon, and choose **Always Allow on This Website** when prompted. You only need the sites you use.

## 3. Sign in, refresh, click

**In Safari**, sign in to [Strava](https://www.strava.com/dashboard) or [Garmin Connect](https://connect.garmin.com/app/newsfeed), **refresh (⌘R)**, then click **Kudo All** at the top right. Garmin: heart beside Upload. The Safari toolbar panel also offers **Give kudos to current feed**.

Only eligible, loaded, unliked activities are processed; scroll to load more, then click again. No automatic scrolling or automatic background kudos.

<details>
  <summary>Need help?</summary>
  <p><strong>No button or disconnected?</strong> Enable the extension, allow the exact website, sign in and refresh. In the Safari toolbar panel, use <strong>Reconnect</strong>; this does not start kudos. Check <strong>Connected</strong> and a positive <strong>Available kudos</strong> count before running. Zero may mean loaded activities are already liked. A hidden toolbar icon can be added through <strong>View → Customize Toolbar</strong>.</p>
  <p>Using a Safari profile? Enable Kudo All for that profile. Private Browsing access is optional.</p>
</details>

<details>
  <summary>Other feeds and results</summary>
  <p>Garmin China: <a href="https://connect.garmin.cn/app/newsfeed">connect.garmin.cn</a> or <a href="https://connectus.garmin.cn/app/newsfeed">connectus.garmin.cn</a>. Legacy <code>/modern/newsfeed</code> also works. Profile and individual activity pages are unsupported. This Mac edition does not support iPhone or iPad.</p>
  <p><strong>Page confirmed</strong> reports the displayed kudos state, not a separate server receipt. Unconfirmed results stop the run; check the page before retrying. Keep the feed open.</p>
</details>

<details>
  <summary>Turn off or uninstall</summary>
  <p>In <strong>Safari → Settings (or Preferences) → Extensions</strong>, clear Kudo All's checkbox to disable it, or choose <strong>Uninstall</strong> and follow the prompt to remove the Mac app. Refresh open feeds. <a href="https://support.apple.com/en-us/102343">Apple's instructions</a>.</p>
</details>
